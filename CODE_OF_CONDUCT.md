# Community conduct

Treat contributors, users and maintainers with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence, welcome different backgrounds and experience levels, and give specific, constructive feedback.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable. These expectations apply to this repository and community spaces where someone represents the project.

Report concerns privately through [Altegio's contact channels](https://alteg.io/en/contact/), identifying this repository and asking for the maintainers. Share only the information needed to investigate. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation in proportion to the behavior. They should explain decisions to affected participants when practical and protect reporters' privacy.
